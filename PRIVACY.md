# AddAd Privacy Policy

AddAd does not collect, transmit, sell or share any user data.

All settings (target websites, whitelist, intensity, redirect URL, theme) are stored locally in your browser using the browser's extension storage and never leave your device. AddAd makes no network requests, contains no analytics or tracking, and shows no real advertisements — all ads are fake and packaged inside the extension.

AddAd only accesses websites you explicitly choose to target.
